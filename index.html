Private Family-Use Application

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Night Clock handles information when the application is used.

Effective date: September 14, 2026

1. About This App

Night Clock is a private application created by DE Family and intended for use by a small, limited group of family members.

It is not designed as a public social network, advertising service, data-broker service, or commercial data-collection platform.

2. Information We Collect

The current version of Night Clock does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, rent, or otherwise monetize personal information.

The app does not require users to provide:

  • Names
  • Home addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Payment information
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Health information
  • Advertising identifiers

If a future version introduces features that collect personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

3. Device Data, Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

Night Clock does not use third-party advertising networks, behavioral advertising, cross-app tracking, or data-broker services.

The app does not intentionally use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

No personal information is sold or shared for targeted advertising purposes.

4. App Permissions

If the application requests an Apple system permission, the permission is used only for the feature described in the iOS or iPadOS permission prompt.

Users can change permissions later through the Settings application on their iPhone or iPad.

The application does not use granted permissions to create advertising profiles or track users across applications or websites.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Because the current version of the application does not intentionally collect personal information, it does not intentionally disclose personal information to advertisers, data brokers, analytics companies, or other third parties.

If third-party services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated to identify the relevant data practices.

Any service provider handling user data on behalf of the application will be expected to protect it consistently with this Privacy Policy and applicable Apple requirements.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The current version of Night Clock does not maintain a developer-operated database containing users' personal information.

Therefore, there is normally no developer-held personal information to retain or delete.

Information stored only on a user's device can generally be removed by deleting relevant application content, resetting the application where such an option exists, or deleting the application from the device.

Questions or deletion requests concerning any information associated with the application may be sent to the contact address below.

7. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where consent is required for a future feature, users will be given an appropriate choice before relevant data is collected or used.

Apple system permissions can be revoked through iOS or iPadOS Settings.

Users may also contact the developer regarding privacy questions, requests, or concerns.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the application and any information it handles.

However, no electronic system, device, network, or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children and Family Use

The application is intended only for a small, known group of family members.

It is not designed to knowingly collect children's personal information for advertising, profiling, marketing, or other commercial purposes.

If a parent or guardian believes that information concerning a child has been provided to the developer, they may contact the developer using the information below.
